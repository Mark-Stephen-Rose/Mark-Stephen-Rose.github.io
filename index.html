About Me

I care about using data to make honest, useful decisions — and about doing it responsibly.

I studied data science with a business minor at Stonehill College, and I now work as a Programmer and Clinical Data Analyst at enGene. Day to day, I write SAS programs to clean and validate clinical trial data, lead patient-level data review sessions, and maintain the Power BI dashboards that study teams use to track data quality and site performance.

I started at enGene as a clinical intern in 2025, grew into a contractor role, and moved into my current position in May 2026. On the side, I still build projects in Python and SQL, from an ASL gesture-recognition model to an AI-powered web scraper, and I keep an eye on the ethical questions that data work always raises.

Outside of work, I'm usually golfing, fishing, playing guitar, or planning the next trip. I'm always happy to talk shop or swap recommendations, so feel free to reach out.
